Components from Iris's video, insights and comparison cards:

- **Card.Section**: a collapsible titled group inside a card whose items sit in one sideways-scrolling row, with
  back/forward buttons when it overflows; `count` and `collapsed`.
- **Stat**: a key number on a muted tile, with a caption.
- **SentimentBar**: positive, neutral and negative segments sized by count, with an optional legend.
- **Tile**: a small muted surface for one item in a row or grid; a link with `href`, a button with `onclick`.
